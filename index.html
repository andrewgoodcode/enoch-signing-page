Enoch signing page

This page does one job: create a Hedera topic and submit one message to it. Signing happens in your HashPack wallet. This page never asks for a seed phrase or a private key, and it has no backend. Nothing is submitted until you tap a button here and approve the transaction in HashPack.

Read before you tap anything. Use Testnet first with a throwaway testnet account. Switch to Mainnet only when the testnet run works and the message below is the one you approved. On Mainnet this spends about $0.01 to create the topic and about $0.0001 to submit the message, paid from the connected wallet.

Setup

A free WalletConnect Cloud project ID is needed for the page to talk to your wallet. Get one at cloud.walletconnect.com. It is a public identifier, not a secret. The page saves it in this browser only, so you do not have to paste it again.

Testnet is for testing only. Mainnet is the real run, and it spends real HBAR.

After you approve in HashPack, switch back to this browser tab yourself with your phone's app switcher. HashPack does not return to the browser on its own when you pair by pasting the pairing string. The page will show the connected account once you are back.

What this page never does


    

  • Never asks for, stores, or sends a seed phrase or a private key.
    • 

  • Never submits a transaction on its own. Each step needs your tap here and your approval in HashPack.
    • 

  • Never talks to a server of ours. The only network calls are to WalletConnect (to reach your wallet), to the public Hedera mirror node (to read the account public key and to confirm results), and to Hedera consensus nodes through your wallet signing session.
